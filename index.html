Questions people ask before trusting a date

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paid tier waiting behind a button. The page is static, so there is nothing to bill for.

Where do the dates come from?

Each rule is written against the agency's own published page — IRS, EDD, CDTFA, FTB, the Secretary of State, or OSHA — and every item links to it. The rules live in one JavaScript file in the public repository, so they can be read and corrected.

Does it store my answers?

In your browser's local storage, so the form is still filled in next time. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing site data clears it.

My state is not California. Is this useful?

Switch the state to "Federal only" and you get the federal payroll and income tax dates, which apply everywhere. Other states are not covered yet and the page says so rather than guessing.

My fiscal year does not end in December. Will this work?

Not properly. The date rules assume a calendar year and a calendar-quarter filing schedule. Use the list as a starting point and confirm the shifted dates with your accountant.

Can I hand this to my accountant?

That is the point of the export. Download the .ics and send it, or print the checklist and mark what is already done. Both carry the agency and source link for each date.
